# Operating model dry run on the current app (2026-11-01)

Shrikant asked us to run the operating model on the app as it stands, to see whether it catches things he
hasn't raised. Build audited: `claude/project-thread-maxlob` at `95506c8` (batch 1 and storyteller pacing).

How: the narration audit (`tools/narration_audit.py`, new) built every scene's transcript at every CPA level
from `content.json` and checked the 5A rules. Then we read the code paths for every screen against
checklists 5B to 5E, including the paths a play-through with presets doesn't take. No simulator run was
possible from the cloud session, so items marked "device check" need a run on the Mac.

Result: 93 automated failures (in 4 kinds) and 41 warnings, and 12 findings in all. 4 were caught by the v1
checklists. 8 got past them and needed new rules (section 3).

## 3. What changes in the operating model (v1.1)

1. **Repeated instructions:** a how-to sentence is said in full at most twice per scene, then in a short form.
   The word cap applies per turn (25 words before she can act) and per sentence (12), not per line.
2. **Edge-path walkthrough:** QA walks these paths on every release, not only the happy path: the time limit
   mid-scene, resume after quitting, replaying a finished place, every chapter unlock, the ear button on every
   screen, and leaving the parent area.
3. **Speech and visuals in step:** any animation that goes with speech (count-along, quick look, highlights)
   is driven by the speech callbacks, never by a fixed timer. QA flags any fixed delay next to speech.
4. **Order of ideas:** no term or symbol before the scene that teaches it, and a vocabulary review of new words.
5. **Deferred items:** every deferred item gets an owner and a line in the change list, and it stays in the
   digest until it's built or Shrikant drops it.
6. **Cloud-runnable audit:** `tools/narration_audit.py` runs on `content.json` in any session (no Mac) at
   gate 4 (script) and in CI. Rules that need run-time behaviour use the app's speech log.
