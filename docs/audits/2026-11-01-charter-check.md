# Charter check on the current app (2026-11-01)

The [product charter](../product-charter.md) applied to all 31 scenes as built (`95506c8`), from each scene's
opening, items and payoff in `content.json`. This is a first pass by the story director and the learning expert.
The child's-eye reviewer and the full rubric scoring come at the `scene-rubric` step of the next batch.

## What's already strong

- **Math is the plot in most scenes (S1).** Stars are woken by counting them, the bridge is rebuilt plank by plank
  from tens, and the feast is shared fairly. These pass both the remove-the-math and the swap tests.
- **CPA and the number line (L2)** run through every chapter, and warm-ups mix earlier skills (L7).
- **The world changes at each payoff (S5),** and every chapter ends with a tease for the next.
- **Kindness wins (S9).** Grub is lonely, not wicked, says sorry, and comes back at the ball.

## What it means

The math engine is sound. The story is a frame around the math more than a story she lives in: she is told what
to do, Sparkle never needs her to think, and nothing connects to her own world. The biggest wins are three
changes that go into every chapter:

1. **A "how did you know?" tap** after a few items per scene (L5).
2. **One Sparkle mistake per chapter** for her to catch (L6).
3. **A named real-life mirror per chapter,** said once in the story and once in the home activity (S2, L8).

The PM will turn these into a chapter-rewrite batch once Shrikant approves the charter.
