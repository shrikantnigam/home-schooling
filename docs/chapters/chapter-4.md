# Chapter 4: The Royal Ball

Process: [chapter-process.md](../chapter-process.md) · Status: built; QA and retro follow the Chapter 3 retro

## 1. Brief (PM)

**Story problem.** The Queen's Royal Ball needs every table to seat the same number of guests, the chairs to stand
in equal rows for the musicians, and the Royal Dance to hop the same number of tiles each time. **The math problem
is the story problem:** the ball can't start until Siara knows how many guests, chairs and tiles there are.

**Math goal.** Multiplication as equal groups, as arrays (rows of the same) and as skip counting on the line.
Products stay at 20 or below, to match the number line and the numeral tiles.

**Skills (4).** `equalGroups`, `arrays`, `skipCount` (by 2, 5 and 10), `multiply` (numerals, "times means groups of").

**Scenes (4).** R1 Seats at the Tables (groups), R2 Rows of Chairs (arrays), R3 The Hopping Dance (skip-count hops),
R4 The Royal Ball (numerals with help).

**Payoff.** The ball begins; the Queen worries the feast won't be shared fairly (teaser for Chapter 5).

**New acceptance criteria.**
- **AC-4a** Every group in an item has the same number of guests, shown in separate circles.
- **AC-4b** In arrays, tapping a row counts by the row size ("4, 8, 12"), not by ones.
- **AC-4c** Skip-count hops draw arcs that each span `b` tiles.
- **AC-4d** No product goes above 20.

## 2. Pedagogy (LE)

| Skill | Concrete | Pictorial | Abstract | Home tip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| equalGroups | Tap every guest, one by one | Tables pictured | "3 groups of 4" | 3 plates of 2 crackers |
| arrays | Tap each row; Sparkle skip counts | Rows pictured | "3 rows of 4" | Buttons in rows |
| skipCount | Hop button, one arc per hop of b tiles | Arcs drawn | "4 hops of 5" | Count shoes by twos |
| multiply | (numerals; help shows the tables) | | "3 × 5" | "3 groups of 4?" in the car |

Concrete equal groups count by ones on purpose: she first sees that the total is a count, then arrays and hops
move her to counting by groups.

**Dose check.** 6 items per skill, about 13 needed under the secure gate. `multiply` is numeral-only, so it starts
at Numbers (retro 2 fix); the others start at Objects.
