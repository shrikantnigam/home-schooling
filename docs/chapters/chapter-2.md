# Chapter 2: The Bridge of Tens

Process: [chapter-process.md](../chapter-process.md) v1 · Status: built, QA in progress

## 1. Brief (PM)

**Story problem.** At the end of the Garden, Siara and Sparkle found an old bridge. Its planks have fallen into
the river. The bridge-keeper beaver stores planks in bundles of ten, and the bridge can only be rebuilt one
span at a time: each span needs the exact number of planks. **The math problem is the story problem:** to
rebuild a span she must know how many planks are there (tens and ones) and how many more are needed.

**Math goal.** Numbers 11 to 20 as "ten and some more", the number line to 20, and adding within 20 by making
ten first (8 + 5 = 8 + 2 + 3).

**Skills (4).**

| ID | Name for the parent | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| `tensOnes` | Ten and some more (11 to 20) | `tensOnes` |
| `line20` | Find numbers to 20 on the line | `findOnLine` (top 20) |
| `bridgeAdd` | Add within 20 by making ten | `bridgeAdd` |
| `hop20` | Add on the number line to 20 | `hop` (top 20) |

**Scenes (5).** B1 Planks in Bundles (`tensOnes`), B2 Stepping Stones to 20 (`line20`), B3 Fill the Gap
(`bridgeAdd`), B4 Long Hops (`hop20`), B5 The Bridge Opens (abstract `bridgeAdd` plus mixed review).

**Payoff.** Each scene adds a span; B5 opens the bridge and the far bank shows a goblin's cave (teaser for
Chapter 3).

**New acceptance criteria.**
- **AC-2a** Every 11 to 20 item shows (concrete) or pictures (pictorial) one bundle of ten plus loose planks, and the prompt says "ten and N more".
- **AC-2b** In `bridgeAdd` concrete, planks fill the first ten-frame before any go into the second, so making ten is visible.
- **AC-2c** Number lines in this chapter run 0 to 20 and every stone has a hit area of at least 60 pt (see deviation D2-1).
- **AC-2d** Chapter 2 is locked on the map until every Garden skill is mastered (or a parent unlocks it).

## 2. Story beats

B1 open: the river, the beaver and the broken bridge. B1 to B4 payoffs: a span snaps into place.
B5 payoff: "You rebuilt the whole bridge! Look, on the other side... is that a cave? I hear someone grumbling about gems."

## 3. Pedagogy (LE)

| Skill | Concrete | Pictorial | Abstract | Home tip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tensOnes | Bundle of ten shown; tap each loose plank, counting on from ten ("eleven, twelve...") | Bundle and planks pictured | "10 and 3" | Bundle 10 straws with a hair tie; add loose straws and count on from ten |
| line20 | Stones 0 to 20, each with its dots | Numbers on every stone | Only 0, 10 and 20 labelled | Chalk a line to 20 and jump to numbers she calls |
| bridgeAdd | Two ten-frames; place planks one by one, first frame fills first | Both frames pictured, two colours | "8 + 5" with help models | 8 spoons in an egg box of 10, add 5 more: how many fill the box first? |
| hop20 | Horseshoe per hop on a 0 to 20 line | Arcs drawn | "12 + 4" on the line | Hop forward on stairs, counting on from where you start |

Hints: model hint lights the empty cells of the first frame (bridgeAdd) or the bundle (tensOnes); worked step
counts on from the larger number; the third try shows the answer and the ten-frame split.

**Dose check.** 4 items reach a level change at best; mastery needs about 18 items per skill. Scenes give
6 to 7 items per skill plus 2 to 3 warm-up uses, so each skill needs about 3 practice scenes over 2+ days.
Same pattern as Chapter 1 (see retro decision on the chapter gate).

## 4. Layout notes (design)

Reuses: corners, numeral tiles (now up to 20, dots in two rows of 5 plus a third row), ten-frame (planks drawn
as flowers' replacement: brown rounded bars), stone line (0 to 20, smaller stones), story panel.
New component: **bundle of ten** (10 bars tied with a band) next to loose bars. River blue background.

## 5. Deviation log

- D2-1: a 0 to 20 line on 1180 pt gives stones about 54 pt wide, under the 60 pt target. Stones keep a 60 pt hit area that overlaps neighbours slightly; tapping the centre is reliable. Ticketed for the illustrated line (two rows or zoom).
