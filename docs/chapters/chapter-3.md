# Chapter 3: The Goblin's Gems

Process: [chapter-process.md](../chapter-process.md) · Status: brief drafted during Chapter 2 QA, built after the Chapter 2 retro

## 1. Brief (PM)

**Story problem.** Across the bridge, a grumpy goblin named Grub is taking the kingdom's gems. Each scene, Siara
works out how many gems are left, where the hops back land, or how many more she has than Grub. **The math
problem is the story problem:** Grub only gives the gems back when she can say exactly how many.

**Math goal.** Subtraction within 20 as taking away, as counting back on the line, and as the difference
between two amounts (how many more), then with numerals.

**Skills (4).** `takeAway` (Take away within 20), `hopBack` (Subtract by hopping back), `compare` (How many more),
`subtract20` (Subtract within 20 with numbers).

**Scenes (5).** K1 Gems Go Missing (take away within 10), K2 Hopping Home (hop back on 0 to 20), K3 Who Has More?
(compare by matching), K4 Take From Ten (take away within 20), K5 The Gems Come Home (numerals with gem help).

**Payoff.** Grub gives the gems back and shows an invitation to a Royal Ball (teaser for Chapter 4).

**New acceptance criteria.**
- **AC-3a** Taken gems stay visible, faded and marked, so she can see both the whole and what is left.
- **AC-3b** In "who has more", the two rows line up one-to-one and only Siara's unmatched gems can be tapped.
- **AC-3c** Hop-back arcs go right to left from the start number.
- **AC-3d** Grub is grumpy but never scary: no shouting lines, no stealing animation, and he says sorry in K5.

## 2. Pedagogy (LE)

| Skill | Concrete | Pictorial | Abstract | Home tip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| takeAway | Tap the gems Grub takes; they fade into his sack | Faded gems pictured | "9 − 4" | Eat 3 of 9 grapes and count what is left |
| hopBack | Hop button moves Sparkle back one stone per hop | Arcs drawn back | "12 − 4" on the line | Step down stairs, counting back |
| compare | Rows matched one-to-one; tap the gems with no partner | Matched rows pictured | "7 and 4: how many more?" | Line up socks against socks |
| subtract20 | (abstract only, help shows the gems) | | "13 − 5" | "13 take away 4" at dinner |

**Dose check.** About 6 items per skill per scene. Under the secure gate (4 of the last 5 at numbers, over 2 days),
each skill needs about 13 items, so about 1 to 2 practice scenes over two days.
