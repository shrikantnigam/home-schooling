# Skill map: which places open when (proposed, for S11)

Today the chapters open in a line: each one waits for the one before it to pass its gate. Proposed: after the
Prologue, a place opens when the skills it needs are secure, and she chooses where to go.

| Place | Teaches | Needs | Opens after |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prologue: Counting Stars (shortened, with a test-out) | Counting, how many, number line to 10, quick look | - | Always open |
| Flower Garden | Adding within 10, bonds, make 10 | Counting | Prologue |
| Fair Feast, part 1 (sharing one by one) | Fair sharing as dealing out | Counting | Prologue |
| Royal Ball, part 1 (equal groups, skip counting by 2, 5, 10) | Equal groups, skip counting | Counting | Prologue |
| Goblin's Cave, part 1 (take away within 10) | Subtracting within 10, compare | Adding within 10 | Garden |
| Bridge of Tens | Teen numbers, adding within 20 | Adding within 10 and make 10 | Garden |
| Goblin's Cave, part 2 (take from ten) | Subtracting within 20 | Teen numbers | Bridge |
| Royal Ball, part 2 (arrays, times) | Multiplying as groups | Skip counting, adding | Ball part 1, Garden |
| Fair Feast, part 2 (dividing with numbers) | Dividing as sharing and grouping | Feast part 1, Ball part 2 | Ball part 2 |
| Moon Crystal | Equal parts, halves, fractions | Fair sharing | Feast part 1 (the finale needs every place's crystal piece) |

The learning expert confirms the prerequisites and the story director rewrites the teasers so that each place
stands on its own. Each place's payoff gives back one piece of the Moon Crystal. The Moon finale needs them all.
