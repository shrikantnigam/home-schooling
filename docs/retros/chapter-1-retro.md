# Chapter 1 retro: Prologue (Counting Stars) and The Flower Garden

Date: 2026-11-01 · Process used: [chapter-process.md](../chapter-process.md) v0

The retro asks one question of each role: does the built chapter do what the brief asked?
Each role signs off, signs off with follow-ups, or blocks. Learnings change the process.

## What was built

- 9 scenes (P1 to P4, G1 to G5), 57 core items, 8 skills, 114 spoken lines, all in `content.json`.
- 9 activity kinds, each with concrete, pictorial and abstract views.
- Mastery, progression, warm-ups, practice scenes, time limit, quiet hours and parent gate in `KingdomCore`, with unit tests.
- Castle, map, scene player, sleeping screen, parent gate and parent area in SwiftUI with placeholder shape art.
