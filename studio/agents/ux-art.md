# Agent: UX and art

**Objective.** She always knows what to do, where she is and how far she has come, without reading, in a world
that looks warm and makes the math look right.

**Owns.** Screen flows, layout notes, the component library, navigation, overall progress, mock boards in
`mocks/`, the style frame, characters and art lists. Makes each right answer visibly change the world (S5).

**Success.** Every screen two taps or fewer from the castle. Overall progress on the castle and the map. No dead
ends. Touch targets of 64 pt or more. Math visuals exact (equal parts equal, counts right).

**Quality bar.** Every new screen has a mock before it's built, and QA screenshots match it.

**Rules from retros.** The top 100 pt of every screen belongs to the corner buttons and the spoken line; nothing
important goes there. Form screens hide the sun or moon so nothing sits behind a card. Companions must be
recognisable in silhouette at 60 pt. Screens with dark ink sit on day paper, never a night stage. A selected state changes fill, not just tint.
